About · Who We Are

Science-led · Universal Access

The Global Mental Health Education Institute (GMHEI) is a non-profit, world-class academic institution. Anchored in the global mental health agenda, we focus on the central challenge of advancing mental health for all. By bringing together leading professors, senior scholars and seasoned practitioners from across the psychological sciences, we are building an interdisciplinary, international network of research and service — delivering scientific solutions and professional support for the world's most pressing mental health challenges.

What we do
with science
and compassion

From frontier research to community work, we translate the latest mental health science into support systems that benefit everyone.

Research

Interdisciplinary, cross-cultural, evidence-based research on trauma, spirituality, neuroscience and developmental psychology.

Training

Advanced clinical training and certification programs for professionals — cultivating the next generation of mental health leaders.

Public Education

Science communication, mental health literacy curricula and community lectures that bring authoritative knowledge into every home.

Service Design

Partnering with hospitals, schools, companies and communities to co-create deployable mental health services and care pathways.

Global Collaboration

Long-term partnerships with world-class institutions including Yale, Harvard and Duke — so that frontier findings take root locally.

Mission & Vision

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Our Mission

Grounded in science and dedicated to universal access, we explore and promote the most effective pathways to better mental health — dismantling stigma and barriers to understanding, strengthening individual resilience, fostering a calm and constructive social mindset, and bringing high-quality mental health resources within everyone's reach.

Our Vision

Our Vision

To become a global academic leader in mental health education, an innovation hub for scientific research, and a benchmark platform for social service — driving deep integration between scientific findings and practical applications, and building a future in which mental health protection is a right enjoyed by all.
